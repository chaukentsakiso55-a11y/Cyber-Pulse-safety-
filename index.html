Safety systems

Current projects and the rules they follow.

01 • GUARDIAN AI

Safer digital spaces

Guardian AI focuses on blocking harmful adult content, supporting safer browsing and giving families understandable protection controls.

Protection should be visible and understandable instead of silently monitoring everything.
02 • COMMUNITY GADGET

Emergency alerts that travel fast

Trusted members can receive emergency alerts through nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi based connections, with alerts forwarded through the local network when available.

Privacy rule: precise location is shared only during an active emergency alert.
03 • STUDYLOCK

Focus with boundaries

StudyLock uses focus sessions and controlled app blocking to reduce distractions while keeping study activity and family controls understandable.

Focus controls should support study goals rather than become hidden surveillance.
04 • COMMUNITY ACCESS

Trusted groups only

Private community codes are used to keep local alert groups separated so people join the community network they are meant to be part of.

Emergency data should only be used for the reason it was shared.
